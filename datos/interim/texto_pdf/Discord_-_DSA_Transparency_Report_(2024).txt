Discord’s Safety team is trained to evaluate and act upon flagged and reported content to enforce our guidelines. Discord also works hard to proactively identify harmful content and conduct so we can remove it and therefore expose fewer users to it. We work to prioritize the highest harm issues such as child safety or violent extremism. We use a variety of techniques and technology to proactively identify harmful behavior, including: · Image hashing and machine-learning powered technologies that help us identify known and unknown child sexual abuse material. In larger communities, we may use automated means to proactively identify harmful content on the platform and enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
